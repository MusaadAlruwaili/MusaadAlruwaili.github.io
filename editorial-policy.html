Editorial Transparency

Editorial Policy

This page explains how engineering articles and resources are created, reviewed, corrected, and separated from advertising.

Updated July 26, 2026Editorial standards

Originality

Articles on this site are written for this website and are intended to provide original educational value. The content draws on engineering study, professional experience, research practice, and practical technical judgment. When outside sources, institutional records, publication pages, or standards are relevant, the page links to those sources where appropriate.

The site does not publish scraped articles, automatically republished content, or guest posts that do not add clear engineering value. Repeated placeholder pages are avoided because they do not help readers or search engines understand the purpose of the site.

Accuracy and review

Engineering content is reviewed for clarity, technical reasonableness, and practical usefulness before publication. Articles are written to be educational and should not replace project-specific engineering design, safety review, employer procedures, utility standards, or local regulations.

Technical topics such as SCADA, embedded systems, power systems, wireless communication, and AI can change over time. Pages may be updated when better explanations, corrections, new project examples, or new research directions become available.

Safety and limitations

Hands-on tutorials are written for learning and prototyping. Electrical work can involve serious risk, especially when mains voltage, industrial equipment, rotating machinery, batteries, or energized cabinets are involved. Readers should use appropriate safety practices and qualified supervision where required.

Any project connected to live electrical systems, industrial controls, safety systems, or public infrastructure should be reviewed by qualified professionals before implementation.

Corrections

If a visitor finds an error, outdated explanation, broken link, or unclear statement, corrections can be requested by email at eng.musaad1@gmail.com. Helpful correction requests include the page title, the section involved, and the reason the content should be revised.

Advertising and sponsorship

This site may use Google AdSense after approval. Advertising content is separate from editorial content. Advertisements do not determine article topics, publication conclusions, research claims, or recommendations. If sponsored material is ever published, it should be clearly identified as sponsored or promotional.
