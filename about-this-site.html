About This Site

This website is the personal engineering knowledge hub of Dr. Musaad Alruwaili.

Updated July 26, 2026Publisher information

Purpose

This site publishes engineering articles, research summaries, project background, and practical learning resources for students, researchers, and industry professionals. The main topics are electrical engineering, smart energy systems, SCADA, industrial automation, IoT, Arduino and ESP32 projects, artificial intelligence, 5G and 6G communication, wireless sensor networks, MATLAB, Python, and academic publishing.

The goal is to make the site useful beyond a traditional CV. A visitor should be able to learn from an article, understand the author's research direction, review peer-reviewed publications, and contact the author for academic or industry collaboration.

Author and publisher

The site is written and published by Dr. Musaad Alruwaili, an electrical engineering researcher with doctoral work in secure and optimized 5G device-to-device communication. The professional background represented on the site includes industrial energy projects, control systems, SCADA, telecommunications, applied research, and engineering education.

External publication links, institutional references, and the downloadable CV are provided to help visitors verify academic and professional context. The site does not represent an employer, university, or publication venue unless a specific page clearly identifies an external source or record.

Audience

The intended audience includes electrical engineering students, graduate researchers, early-career engineers, educators, project engineers, utility and industrial professionals, and collaborators interested in smart infrastructure. Articles are written to connect theory with practice, especially where research meets field engineering.

Contact

For collaboration, corrections, consulting inquiries, speaking invitations, graduate supervision discussions, or questions about content on this site, use the contact form on the homepage or email eng.musaad1@gmail.com.
