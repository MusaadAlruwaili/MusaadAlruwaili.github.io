Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when visitors use this website.

Updated October 29, 2026Privacy and advertising

Information visitors provide

When you submit the contact form, your browser sends the form fields, including your name, email address, collaboration type, and message, directly to FormSubmit over HTTPS for delivery to the site owner by email. This site does not operate its own contact-submission database. FormSubmit and the email provider process the information through their services. If submission fails, the page offers an email link that can open your email application with a prepared message. Contact details are used to respond to your inquiry. Avoid including confidential information in the form.

If you contact Dr. Musaad Alruwaili directly by email or WhatsApp, the information you send is handled through those services and used to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies and advertising

This site includes Google AdSense code. If AdSense is approved and ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, depending on visitor settings, location, and applicable law.

Visitors can learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use Google services at Google's partner sites policy page. Visitors can also manage ad personalization through their Google account and browser settings.

Language preferences and translation

Language controls may store preferredSiteLanguage or siteLanguage in browser local storage. The Google Translate option also sets a googtrans language cookie with a default lifetime of one year and loads a Google translation script when translation is requested or a saved Arabic preference is restored. These values are language preferences, not login tokens. Dedicated Arabic article pages can be read without requesting automatic translation.

You can clear saved preferences and cookies in browser settings. Blocking third-party scripts may affect translation, advertising, and analytics. The contact form uses FormSubmit; review its current service information before sending personal data.

External links

The site links to external pages such as IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar, ORCID, ProQuest, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and other academic or professional resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by this website.

Analytics and hosting

The website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data required to deliver the website. The pages also load Metricool’s website tracking script for traffic analytics. This causes your browser to contact Metricool. For details about its handling of technical and usage information, data retention, and cookies, see Metricool’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact for privacy questions

For privacy questions, correction requests, or requests about information sent through the contact form, email eng.musaad1@gmail.com.
