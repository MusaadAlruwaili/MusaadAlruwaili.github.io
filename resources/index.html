Learning Paths

Suggested paths through the library.

For students

Start with the ESP32 relay tutorial to understand how software controls hardware. Then read the SCADA guide to see how field devices, controllers, telemetry, and operator screens work together in larger systems. Finally, compare MATLAB and Python so you can choose tools for lab reports, simulations, and data analysis.

For graduate researchers

Begin with the IEEE writing guide and use it as a checklist for your next manuscript. Then read the AI smart-grid article and the AI in 6G article to see how engineering research questions can connect algorithms, validation, security, and infrastructure impact.

For industry engineers

Use the SCADA and smart-grid articles as a practical bridge between operations and research. The goal is to identify where data, communication, automation, and AI can improve monitoring, maintenance, reliability, and decision support without losing sight of safety and field constraints.
